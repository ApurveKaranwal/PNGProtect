Why Clean Metadata?

Hidden Data Dangers

Photos taken with modern cameras and smartphones contain a treasure trove of hidden data called EXIF data. This can include:

  • 📍 GPS Coordinates: Exact location where the photo was taken.
  • 📅 Timestamps: Exact date and time used.
  • 📱 Device Info: Phone model and serial numbers.

Privacy First

Sharing a raw photo online is like sharing your location history. Aggressive metadata stripping removes 100% of this hidden information, leaving only the visual pixel data.

This is crucial for privacy-conscious users, whistleblowers, or anyone who simply doesn't want big tech tracking their movement patterns through their photo uploads.
