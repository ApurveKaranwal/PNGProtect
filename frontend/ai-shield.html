About AI Shield Protection

Defending Against Scrapers

Generative AI models are trained on billions of images scraped from the internet, often without the consent of the original artists. AI Shield provides a layer of defense against this mass scraping.

Adversarial Perturbations

AI Shield works by injecting adversarial perturbations—essentially "noise" designed mathematically to confuse machine learning models.

While a human sees a "cat", the slight alterations in pixel values might trick an AI into seeing "toaster" or just nonsense static. This effectively "poisons" the data for training purposes, making your artwork useless to scrapers.

Protection Levels

  • 30% (Low): Minimal changes. Best for high-fidelity photography where visual purity is paramount.
  • 50% (Standard): The best balance. Hard for AI to process, but still looks great to humans.
  • 80%+ (High): Aggressive protection. May introduce visible grain, but offers maximum resistance against robust scraping models.
Protects against: ResNet, CLIP, ViT, and other common computer vision architectures used in Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, etc.
