Ownership Verification

Blind Verification

Our verification process is "blind"—it doesn't need the original image to find the watermark. This allows anyone to verify an image found in the wild without needing access to your private archives.

Cryptographic Confidence

Verification isn't just a "yes/no". We provide a Confidence Score. If an image has been heavily compressed (like on social media) or cropped, the watermark signal might be weaker. The score tells you how likely it is that the detected ID is genuine.

On-Chain Registry

For ultimate proof, ownership claims can be registered on the Blockchain (Ethereum/Polygon).

When you "Register Ownership On-Chain", we create a permanent, immutable record linking your watermarked image's hash to your wallet address. This provides court-admissible proof of existence at a specific point in time.
